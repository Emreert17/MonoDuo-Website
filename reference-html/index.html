04 / A few questions

Before we
begin.

Who is MonoDuo for?

Creators with an engaged audience and expertise that can help solve a specific problem. We begin by exploring whether a digital product makes sense for that audience.

Do I need a product idea already?

No. Audience research is part of the process. If you already have an idea, we can explore how it connects to the questions your audience is asking.

What kind of products can we explore?

A focused ebook, guide, workbook, or practical resource can be a starting point. We choose the format around the problem, rather than deciding on a format first.

How does the partnership work?

We discuss scope, responsibilities, timing, and commercial terms before starting. Each collaboration needs a clear agreement that fits the creator and the project.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Our first pilot reached three paying customers. Research helps us make informed decisions, but a launch is also a way to test demand and learn.
